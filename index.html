Frequently asked questions

The practical details.

A few useful things to know before starting a project.

Are the website prices once-off?

Yes. Website development is a once-off fee. Domain registration and optional monthly care are separate costs.

Do I have to pay monthly maintenance?

No. Website maintenance is optional. You can purchase a website without a monthly care package.

Can I buy maintenance without a website?

Our listed care plans are designed for websites developed by Andrena. Existing third-party websites would require a separate assessment and quotation.

Do I own my domain?

We recommend registering the domain in your business’s name, even where Andrena assists with purchasing and setup. Domain registration and renewals are quoted separately.

What do I need to provide?

Your business information, services, contact details, images, logo and any content relevant to the agreed website.

How long will my website take?

Typical targets after all required content is received are 2–3 working days for Starter, 3–5 for Standard and 5–7 for Premium. Final timing depends on scope and review.

How do payments work?

Our proposed standard arrangement is a 50% deposit to begin and the remaining 50% before launch. We will confirm the terms in your quotation.

What about custom features?

Simple interactive features are included within reasonable package scope. Payments, databases, user accounts, complex bookings and advanced integrations require a separate quote.
